Knowing Ahmedabad

What families here
often weigh.

Every family is different. These are common considerations, not assumptions about you.

Community and language

For some families, community and mother tongue are central; for others, practice and character matter more. Silarah lets you set those preferences openly, so nobody has to guess what the other side expects.

Business and professional families

Trade, family business and professional careers are common in Gujarat. Being clear about work, finances and where you plan to live helps introductions move forward with fewer surprises.

Across Gujarat and abroad

Introductions may connect Ahmedabad with Vadodara, Surat, Bharuch, Rajkot or relatives abroad. Distance and location preferences let you decide how far to look.

Safety first

The first meeting

Meet first in a busy café or family restaurant, or with family present, and let someone you trust know your plans. If your family prefers to speak first, connect a guardian in Silarah so their involvement is clear to everyone.

Before you share anything personal

Keep conversations in the app until trust develops. Never send money, OTP codes or identity documents, and report anyone who asks. Read the Safety Centre for more.

Prefer a marriage bureau?

Some families use a local bureau or a relative to suggest proposals. Silarah works differently: you set your own preferences, nobody sees your phone number, and there is no agent fee. Read marriage bureau or matrimony app? to compare.
