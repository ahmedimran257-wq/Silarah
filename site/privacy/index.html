Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Silarah processes personal data to provide and protect the app, website and related support, safety and verification services.

1. Data fiduciary and contact

Silarah is operated in India and is the data fiduciary responsible for deciding why and how personal data is processed. Privacy questions and rights requests: privacy@silarah.com. The Silarah Grievance Desk receives formal grievances at grievance@silarah.com.

2. Data you provide

  • Account identifiers, verified email and authentication information.
  • Date of birth, gender, marital and family information, city, region, country and optional approximate location.
  • Education, occupation, income range, lifestyle, biography, preferences and matrimonial timeline.
  • Religious information such as sect, practice and Islamic identity choices.
  • Profile photos and temporary guided photo-verification captures.
  • Interests, bookmarks, private shortlist categories, private notes and reminder dates, messages, blocks, reports, support communications and guardian details.
  • If you use voice calling, we also collect your call settings (Ring, Silent or Calls off, quiet hours, and calls you mute or block from a particular member), the calls you plan with a match, and any report you make from a call.
  • If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address, profile picture and Google account identifier with Silarah when you sign in. We use the verified email address to create your account or sign you in, and keep these details with your login record. We do not use your Google name or picture for your Silarah profile, and we do not access your contacts, files or any other Google account data.

Shortlist details are visible only to the account that writes them. Do not put passwords, OTPs, payment details, government identifiers or another person’s confidential contact information in notes.

3. Data generated through use

We process consent and authentication records, device and app version, language, settings, push tokens, feature activity, matches, message delivery and typing state, moderation and fraud signals, subscription and transaction events, crash diagnostics, security information, timestamps and audit logs. Approximate device location is requested only for radius discovery and can be denied. When voice calling is available to you, we process call records: who called whom, when, the result (answered, declined, missed or not reached), how long you talked and the call minutes used; the time a ring reached your phone; whether your phone can ring (notification permission and the incoming-call setting); your call-minute balance, minute-pack purchases, Google Play refunds of packs and any balance below zero; and call-quality diagnostics sent by the calling software, such as connection statistics and the device model.

4. Sensitive and verification data

Religious beliefs, temporary verification captures and some matrimonial information may be sensitive. Religious data is used for compatibility with explicit consent where required. The optional photo check creates three temporary guided captures for human comparison with the current profile photo. We do not create facial embeddings or templates, perform government-ID matching or estimate age from a face. Sensitive data is not used for third-party advertising.

5. Purposes and legal bases

We use data to secure accounts; provide discovery, explainable compatibility, interests, chat, private shortlists and reminders, Incognito, photo privacy, guardian and subscription features; personalize results; moderate and verify; deliver service and safety messages; prevent fraud; support members; diagnose failures; keep records; and comply with law. Compatibility uses stated profile and preference fields and returns criterion-level explanations rather than another member’s raw preference values. For processing in India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act when its relevant provisions apply, we rely on consent or a specific statutory legitimate use under section 7 whose conditions are met. A contract or a general business or safety interest is not, by itself, a DPDP legal basis. Any processing or retention required or authorised by another applicable Indian law is limited to that law and purpose. Other jurisdictions may confer additional rights. You may withdraw consent prospectively; we will explain any resulting feature limits and any processing that law requires or permits to continue.

6. Member and guardian visibility

Chosen profile fields are visible to eligible registered members under discovery and privacy settings. Incognito removes a profile from general discovery and name/city search while it is effective, but existing interests, matches, permitted photo-access relationships and connected guardian relationships remain accessible as needed. It cannot make prior interactions anonymous. Photos follow public, mutual-interest or request-to-view controls. Private shortlist categories, notes and reminders are visible only to their author and are not shown to the saved member or their guardian. Conversation messages are visible to participants and may be visible to a properly linked guardian where chat mirroring is enabled. Reports expose necessary information only to authorized safety staff and those required for a fair process.

7. Voice calls

What calling is. Matched members may call each other inside Silarah without sharing phone numbers. Calling is optional; you can use Silarah without it and set Calls off at any time.

Microphone. Silarah asks for microphone access when you place or answer a call and uses the microphone only while a call is in progress. You can withdraw the permission in your phone’s settings.

No recording. Your voice is sent live between the two phones through our calling provider. Calls are never recorded, transcribed or stored by Silarah or the provider, no member can turn recording on, and Silarah staff cannot listen to calls. Reports and safety reviews use call records (times, results and length), never call audio.

What the other member sees. When you call, the other member’s phone shows your name and your photo as your photo settings allow, including on the incoming-call screen over their lock screen, so they can decide whether to answer. Missed-call and other call notifications on a lock screen hide the name when the phone’s settings hide private notification content.

What a caller can learn. Whether a ring reached the other member’s phone (the call shows “Calling…” until it does and “Ringing…” after), and, during the other member’s quiet hours, that it is their quiet hours and when calls will ring again. Other reasons a call does not ring, such as calls turned off, Silent, or calls from that caller muted or blocked, are never revealed; the caller sees the same neutral message.

Protection from unwanted calls. If calls to the same member go unanswered too many times in a day (the number is shown in the app), counting calls that reached their phone and calls they refused through their own settings, the caller cannot call that member again until the next day. Calls to a phone that was offline or in quiet hours do not count. You can report a member from the call screen.

Guardians. A properly linked guardian sees the time and length of a member’s calls, never their content.

Legal basis. We process call data with your consent, which you give when you accept the calling terms and choose to call or receive calls. You can withdraw it by setting Calls off; call records already made are then kept only as described under Retention.

8. Processors and disclosures

We use Supabase for verified-email authentication (including sign-in with Google), database, private storage, realtime and server functions; Google Firebase for Play Integrity-backed App Check, push delivery and crash reporting; Google for Continue with Google sign-in, where you choose it; RevenueCat and app stores for subscriptions; Brevo for transactional email; Cloudflare for web delivery, DNS and security; Google ML Kit for on-device capture guidance and photo-safety signals; and Photon/Wikidata for location and language lookup. Chat translation is no longer offered and we do not send new messages to a translation provider. They process data needed for their role under their own obligations. We may also disclose data at your direction, during a corporate transaction, for safety, or when lawfully required. We do not sell personal data or run third-party behavioural advertising. Stream (Stream.io, Inc.) provides real-time voice-call transport and the call records it needs to run a call. It receives your Silarah member identifier and your first name with initial, and relays call audio without recording it. Call minute packs, like subscriptions, are sold through Google Play with RevenueCat; Silarah never sees your card or bank details.

9. International processing

Providers may process data outside your country. Where required, we use contractual safeguards and assess provider security and transfer mechanisms. Contact us for information relevant to your region. Call audio and call data are routed through Stream’s servers in India; Stream may access data from other countries under its contractual and security obligations.

10. Retention

Active-account profile, preference, photo, match and message data remains while needed to provide Silarah. Private shortlist metadata is removed with its bookmark or account; a one-shot reminder is marked sent rather than repeatedly queued. Incognito preference metadata remains with the active account so the setting is enforced across devices. Deletion requests enter a 30-day recovery period. Temporary photo-verification captures are deleted from active verification storage after review and no later than 48 hours after submission; the decision and deletion audit may remain without the captures. Information and removed content that applicable intermediary rules require us to preserve may be retained for 180 days, or longer under a lawful order. Reports, blocks, transaction, consent, security and backup records otherwise remain only for applicable payment, tax, abuse-prevention, limitation and legal periods, then are deleted or de-identified. Backups expire through normal rotation. In-app Privacy requests, responses and associated case records are retained while unresolved and for 12 months after resolution, then erased from active systems unless a documented legal hold applies. Holds are access-restricted and reviewed; after a hold ends, an already-expired case becomes eligible for erasure. Unlinking a deleted account does not anonymise the request text. Backup copies expire through provider rotation. This case-retention schedule does not extend retention of the underlying profile or authorise unrelated processing. Call records (who called whom, when, the result, talk time and minutes) are kept for 12 months and then deleted, except while an open report or a lawful order needs them longer. They are deleted with your account, except records that law requires us to preserve, which are deleted when that period ends. Removing a call from your history hides it from you only; the record is kept for the same period for safety. Minute-pack purchase and refund records are kept as transaction records for the periods tax and payment law require. Call audio is never recorded or stored. Google sign-in details are kept with your login record and deleted with your account.

11. Security

Controls include encrypted transport, private temporary-capture storage, row-level permissions, short-lived signed media access, restricted staff roles, audit logs, rate limits, backups and managed secrets. No system is risk-free. Protect OTP codes and report suspected compromise promptly. We investigate suspected personal-data breaches, take proportionate containment and remediation steps, and notify affected people and the competent authority within the time and manner required by applicable law. Call audio is encrypted in transit between your phone and our calling provider. It is not end-to-end encrypted: it passes through the provider’s servers to reach the other member, where it is relayed and not stored.

12. Your rights and choices

Depending on law, you may access, correct, export or delete data; withdraw consent; object or restrict processing; request portability; nominate another person where applicable; and complain to a regulator. In-app controls support profile editing, photo visibility, pausing, notification settings, blocking, immediate download of a machine-readable ZIP archive and deletion. We may proportionately verify identity before acting. See the Privacy Rights & Grievance Policy or email privacy@silarah.com. Signed-in members can open Profile → Settings → Privacy → Privacy requests for assisted access, correction, erasure, withdrawal, nomination or grievance requests and read the reference, response target and staff response there. On older app versions without this screen, or if you cannot sign in, email privacy@silarah.com; urgent safety complaints should use safety@silarah.com or the reporting tools. A withdrawal or erasure submission creates a request for staff review; it does not immediately stop processing or delete your account. Pausing discovery only limits visibility. Use existing account controls where available, or specify the consent and purpose you want to withdraw through Privacy requests or privacy@silarah.com. After proportionate verification, we will explain and carry out the applicable cessation and processor follow-up, subject to processing required or authorised by law. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing and may make a dependent feature unavailable. Store subscriptions must be cancelled separately. Selecting Nomination submits a request to arrange and verify a nomination for exercise of rights on death or incapacity where applicable; it does not appoint a nominee or complete a nomination. Staff must explain the process, verify necessary authority and confirm any completed arrangement separately. Do not put nominee identity documents or unnecessary third-party information in the request. Your data export includes your call history and a summary of your call minutes (packs bought, refunded and used). You can turn calls off, set Silent, mute or block calls from a member and change your quiet hours at any time. If you signed in with Google, you can remove Silarah’s access in your Google Account under third-party connections; you can still sign in with an email code.

13. Adults only

Silarah is for adults aged 18 and over. If you believe a minor supplied data, contact safety@silarah.com.

Material changes will be notified in-app or by email and renewed consent requested where required. This page’s version and effective date identify the applicable notice.
