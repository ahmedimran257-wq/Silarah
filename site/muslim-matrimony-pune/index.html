Knowing Pune

What families here
often weigh.

Every family is different. These are common considerations, not assumptions about you.

Long-settled families and newcomers

Some members grew up in Pune; others arrived for college or IT and engineering jobs and may plan to move again. Saying where you expect to settle helps both sides judge fit early.

Careers and timelines

Working professionals often weigh careers, work hours and relocation alongside faith and family. Being clear about your timeline for marriage helps avoid mismatched expectations.

Pune, Mumbai and beyond

Introductions may connect Pune with Mumbai, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Solapur or families in other states. Distance and location preferences let you decide how far to look.

Safety first

Meeting in
Pune.

The first meeting

Meet first in a busy café or family restaurant and let someone you trust know your plans. If your family prefers to speak first, connect a guardian in Silarah so their involvement is clear to everyone.

Before you share anything personal

Keep conversations in the app until trust develops. Never send money, OTP codes or identity documents, and report anyone who asks. Read the Safety Centre for more.

Prefer a marriage bureau?

Some families use a local bureau or a relative to suggest proposals. Silarah works differently: you set your own preferences, nobody sees your phone number, and there is no agent fee. Read marriage bureau or matrimony app? to compare.
