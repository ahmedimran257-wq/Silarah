Refund Policy

Silarah subscriptions and call minute packs are non-refundable. We refund only our own mistakes (a double charge, a wrong amount, or a fault on our side) and charges you did not authorise.

1. Purchases are final

Subscription payments and call minute packs are final and non-refundable once the purchase is completed, including for any unused part of a billing period and any unused minutes. The price, billing period, renewal terms and pack size are shown by Google Play and on the purchase screen before you pay. This Policy does not limit any statutory right that cannot be excluded under applicable consumer law.

2. When we do not refund

We do not refund because you changed your mind, did not find a match, did not receive interests, replies or calls, did not use Premium or your minutes, a member did not answer or ended a call, found a member incompatible, became engaged or married, forgot to cancel before a renewal, uninstalled the app, paused or deleted your account, or lost access after we enforced the Terms of Service or Community Guidelines.

3. When we do refund

We refund only when: you were charged more than once for the same subscription period or the same pack; a charge is confirmed as not authorised by you; you were charged a different amount from the price shown at purchase; minutes you paid for were never added to your account and we could not add them within a reasonable time after you reported it; or Premium did not work for a substantial part of the paid period because of a fault on Silarah’s side that we could not fix within a reasonable time after you reported it. The refund covers the duplicate, unauthorised or excess amount, the missing pack, or the affected part of the period, and is paid through Google Play to the original payment method. If a fault on our side used call minutes wrongly, for example on a call that never connected, we give those minutes back to your balance.

4. How call minutes are used

Only the caller’s minutes are used, and only for time both members were connected, counted per started minute. A call that does not connect uses no minutes. Premium’s included minutes are used before pack minutes. Pack minutes do not expire, but you need active Premium to use them; if Premium ends, they are kept for when you renew.

5. Google Play purchases and refunds

Payments are processed by Google Play, not by Silarah, and Silarah never sees your card or bank details. Google Play may independently grant a refund under its own policies; for example, Google offers a refund request flow for many purchases made within 48 hours. Those decisions are made by Google and are outside this Policy. When a purchase is refunded, by Google or by us, what it paid for is removed: a refunded subscription ends that Premium access and removes that period’s included call minutes; a refunded pack removes that pack’s minutes from your balance. If you had already used those minutes, your pack balance goes below zero and you cannot place calls, with pack or included minutes, until you add minutes; the next pack you buy first makes up the difference. If Google reverses a refund, the minutes come back.

6. How to request an eligible refund

Email support@silarah.com from your Silarah account email, preferably within 30 days of the charge, with the Google Play order ID (it begins with GPA.), the charge date and a short description. We may ask for proportionate evidence, such as a screenshot showing both charges. Never send card numbers, bank details, passwords or OTP codes. Later requests are considered where the law requires it.

7. Cancellation and renewal

You can cancel at any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions, or through Manage Subscription in the app. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Premium until the end of the period you already paid for, and that period is not refunded. Uninstalling the app, pausing a profile or deleting a Silarah account does not cancel Google Play billing. A free trial, if offered, becomes a paid subscription unless cancelled before the deadline shown.

8. Account deletion and pause

Paid call minutes remaining when an account is deleted are lost and are not refunded; the app shows how many you have before you confirm deletion. Pausing a profile keeps them.

9. Changes to Premium or calling

We may add, change or retire individual Premium features, including calling, as the Service develops. If a change materially reduces what you paid for during an active period, or calling is withdrawn while you hold paid pack minutes, we will offer a reasonable remedy, such as an equivalent feature, extra time or a proportionate refund, where applicable law requires it.

10. Contact

Billing questions: support@silarah.com. Formal complaints: grievance@silarah.com. Nothing in this Policy affects non-waivable statutory rights, including under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
