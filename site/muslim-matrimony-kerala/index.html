Knowing Kerala

What families here
often weigh.

Every family is different. These are common considerations, not assumptions about you.

Families between Kerala and the Gulf

Many Kerala families have members working in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar or Oman. Being clear about where you live now, where you plan to settle and whether you expect to move helps both sides judge fit early.

Education and careers

Education is highly valued, and many members work in healthcare, engineering, teaching and business. Sharing qualifications and plans plainly helps introductions start on solid ground.

Across the districts

Introductions may connect Malappuram, Kozhikode, Kannur, Kasaragod, Thrissur and Kochi. Distance and location preferences let you decide how far to look.

Safety first

Meeting in
Kerala.

The first meeting

Meet first in a busy public place or with family present, and let someone you trust know your plans. If your family prefers to speak first, connect a guardian in Silarah so their involvement is clear to everyone.

Before you share anything personal

Keep conversations in the app until trust develops. Never send money, OTP codes or identity documents, and report anyone who asks. Read the Safety Centre for more.

Prefer a marriage bureau?

Some families use a local bureau or a relative to suggest proposals. Silarah works differently: you set your own preferences, nobody sees your phone number, and there is no agent fee. Read marriage bureau or matrimony app? to compare.
